# Real demonstration product photographs

38 real photographs illustrate 45 fictional listings. Some photos are reused for related products. Book covers, packaging, quantities, colors and exact models are illustrative. No AI image generation was used.

These photographs retain their individual Creative Commons/public-domain terms. Source author, license URL and source URL are also copied into the product description and the deployed public `demo/catalog/photos/credits.html`. The photographs were resized by Wikimedia's thumbnail service; no generative editing or content alteration was applied.
