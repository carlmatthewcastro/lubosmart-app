[.agents/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md](.agents/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

or `mobile/`.

before frontend work and use the applicable skills in `.agents/skills/`.

`.agents/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`; reusable workflows live in `.agents/skills/`.
